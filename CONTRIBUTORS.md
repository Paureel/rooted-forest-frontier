## Sources and assistance

The project uses the BootLoops toolkit and companion research protocols under
their original MIT notices. Retrieved scientific sources retain their original
authors and rights. Read the source-specific audit ledgers and manifests before
attributing an algorithm or theorem. Codex assisted with research recorded in
the archive and with repository preparation.

The collaboration organization was inspired by CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds,
maintained by Douglas Colkitt. Its integer-multiplication research code and
results are outside this project. Add contributor credit with the relevant PR
and evidence; include independent reviews and useful failed/superseded work.
