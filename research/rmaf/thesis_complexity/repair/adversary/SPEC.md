# Adversary check specification (frozen before search)

Claim family: two-active-leaf local repair in `../.. /thesis_complexity_review/local-repair.md` (actual allowed path recorded in README). A full in-regime fire must satisfy source depth orientation, branch priority, protected-edge legality, unary labelled rho, and stated local history/premises. A locally valid state without a proven source call path is only a geometric-lemma test.

Instrument: independent graph-based rooted forest manipulation. No imports of project evaluators. Incoming protection is a boolean attached to each vertex's parent edge; label sets identify active contracted roots. Rho is retained. Source pair selection maximizes F1 depth within deepest protection eligibility, then separate-component priority, then F2 deepest endpoint. All tied choices may be inspected. All branches are examined regardless of success values.

Controls before search: frozen five controls, plus exact identity/null cuts, protected-edge rejection, parent-protection inheritance, and root-sibling exclusion. Comparator must reject a changed pendant count and a changed protection label. All source-listed predicates are considered before A2/A3. Failure of any control stops search.

Bounded pilots: first 100 locally sampled 6–8-leaf trees, then a timed larger sample if controls pass. Work unit = source-legal local G4.1 triples whose C outcome is checked, with count and witnesses retained. Stop on first in-regime fire. No finite no-fire licenses a theorem or asymptotic complexity conclusion.

History attack: examine legal cuts that neither remove active a nor merge it, with its sibling incoming edge protected. Counterexample criterion: after simultaneous legal cuts and suppression, a has no protected sibling (unless a ceases to be active). Enumerate finite local trees/cut sets independently of recurrence credit assignments.

Arithmetic: exact rational allowances and Lean checked conditional inequalities. Alpha>=128 with elementary 2^(K-3)>=1/8 for K>=0 covers dead C; empty-credit induction is imported only for smaller cut budgets. No fixed-alpha finite call count refutes an asymptotic assertion with existential alpha.
