See `history-counterexample.md` and `fresh_review/new_history/ADDENDUM.md`.

**Inside-sibling continuation — new unresolved case.** For every reachable exceptional G4.1 state whose selected retained endpoint is inside the inherited protected sibling b, construct a complete legal C prefix satisfying the same coefficient/intercept interface. The new witness has a two-call C subtree and therefore does not refute this bound. Its G3 A2 endpoint cut leaves both children of b protected; a general proof must track the corresponding surviving protected subtrees through earlier reductions rather than import the false outside-b assertion. This is a candidate case distinction, not an established universal invariant.

**Legal-prefix construction.** Under explicit local-state hypotheses, construct a finite prefix containing every legal C continuation until terminal invocations or empty-credit induction exits. Prove the normalization, no-free-merger, retained-sibling, and double-protected-distance statements for the actual source tree representation. For a prefix with V expanded calls, L exits and decreases d measured from C, the recurrence route accepts the exact affine allowance test. A sufficient envelope is `sum(2^-d)<=1/4` and `V-3L<=2`. A larger intercept can also be offset by strict coefficient slack at the stated alpha and budget.

**A continuation.** The target composition also requires `I(A)<=alpha*2^(K-2)-2`. This is a nonempty-credit continuation claim. The outer source induction explicitly grants empty-credit smaller-budget bounds, so the A allowance requires its own group argument, a valid stronger induction with its state hypotheses, or an equivalent prefix certificate. The B child is empty-credit by the G4.1 reset, subject to explicit terminal-budget handling.

**Annotation closure.** Specify how the proposed frontier reset changes the analysis metadata, then prove every legal history receives valid annotations and every invoked allowance has its required hypotheses. Include the reached P={a,x,u}, Q={x,u} state, trivial mergers/root removals, and all negative-budget failures. Neither extending p to three nor checking a finite coefficient table proves this closure.

**Count-to-time theorem.** Even a completed joint G4 count lemma leaves the complete global induction, the source's per-invocation O(n) implementation claim, and any rho/unaugmented parameter transfer to their separately stated proofs. No conclusion from the separate correctness/transfer arms is imported here.
