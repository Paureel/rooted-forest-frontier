## Conditional count calculation

`I(C)<=X/8+2`

suffices for

`1+I(A)+I(B)+I(C)<=X/2-2`.

The recurrence route proves this exact interface in Lean. The initially proposed sufficient C bound with additive -1 is three calls stronger than needed. This calculation does not establish the A or C structural allowances.

The same route formalizes a finite-prefix interface. Expand every legal C continuation until terminal invocations or empty-credit induction exits. If V invocations are expanded, L exits remain, and d records their budget decreases from C, then the imported empty-credit bounds give the majorant

`alpha*2^(K-1)*sum(2^-d)+V-3L`.

The conditions `sum(2^-d)<=1/4` and `V-3L<=2` suffice. The exact formal interface can also use intercept slack when the coefficient is strictly smaller. Normalizations add no invocation; actual negative-budget children are terminal calls, including budgets below the source's displayed -3 base range.
