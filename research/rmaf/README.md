# Rooted MAF campaign

Started 2026-10-14. The target and acceptance criteria are in `spec.json`.
Progress records under `progress/` are evidence-backed checkpoints consumed
by the local dashboard. Each search route owns its own record and journal.
Open, conditional, tested, refuted, and Lean-checked claims are distinct.

Toolkit consultation: the BootLoops index and capability catalog contain
exact arithmetic and research protocols but no rooted-MAF forest enumerator
or Lean adapter. This project supplies the problem-specific instruments;
the dashboard extends its existing local observer rather than forking it.

Small-instance tests are discovery and bug-finding evidence, not general
proofs. No improved exponential bound is claimed at campaign initialization.
