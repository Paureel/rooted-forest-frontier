## Exact accounting and normal-form checks

`proof_scope_checks.py` ran after the existing control battery. Its values had already been stated in the proof or author receipt, so the results are post-hoc arithmetic and semantics checks, not held-out acceptance data. The retained receipt is `research/rmaf/results/triple-dp-verification-proof-scope.json`.

The independent partition/path oracle gives three components for the original quartet and three for its a-cut child. Removing the completed singleton gives a live three-leaf problem with two components. The paid-cut total is 1+(2−1)=2; the full partition has 2+1=3 components. This supplies the exact witness for the missing distinction between p_live and p_total. The source child also matches the restriction after deleting a, and all four target/source cherry tests have exactly two pendant arcs; the example triggers neither prior t=1 nor prior t≥3 Whidden case.

For the normal-form issue, use target ((a,b),(c,d)) with separate leaf blocks. One root-open a-region has vertices {root, left child, a}; another adds the right child but no right-subtree leaf. Both are connected, disjoint from the b/c/d singleton regions, and induce identical leaf blocks. Trimming the leafless right-child incursion maps the second to the first. The witness does not contradict the solver; it shows that a bijection on unrestricted support choices requires a normal-form restriction or quotient.
