## Claims checked against permitted artifacts

Its elapsed time, empty failure list, finite PASS, and theorem OPEN agree with WORK.md. The pilot totals 389 cases, including 20 at n=5, and its recorded n=5 time agrees with the prose.

The full receipt records the same oracle and production control counts. Oracle, controls, and harness hashes agree across controls, pilot, and full receipts; the pilot and full solver hashes agree. The timestamps are consistent with controls preceding pilot and full. These observations are not an independent proof that no earlier production access occurred.

It does not itself retain a source hash, invocation exit status, or the reported source-grep results. The precise Lean statements and their source locations were not independently checked in this restricted audit.

One LOW-severity precision issue remains in WORK.md:33: “all generator/control/harness/solver SHA256 values” does not match the receipt's field names, which are `oracle_sha256`, `controls_sha256`, `harness_sha256`, and `solver_sha256`. This is an ambiguity, not an established missing-integrity defect. Similarly, the pilot receipt alone does not document the sampling procedure or seed, so its “uniformly sampled” description was not independently verified here.
