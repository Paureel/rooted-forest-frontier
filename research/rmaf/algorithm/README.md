# Main complete-algorithm research

The target is a complete exact ordinary rooted MAF algorithm with a rigorous
smaller exponential base. All three milestones are open. Read TARGET.md and
PIN.md before working here; WORKFILE-2026-10-14.md is the coordinator record.

The staged campaign is X0 adversary first, then two distinct fresh-context
design routes (joint branch/state macro and paid decomposition/safe cover),
then a separate nonauthor executable/Lean/interface review. X0 fixtures may
guide design; after that contact they cannot serve as held-out certification
of the chosen design. Fresh verification must retain that provenance.

Code here is problem-specific. The BootLoops capability index was consulted:
the installed tools provide algebraic/numerical claim checks and phylogenetic
likelihoods, rather than exact rooted agreement-forest cut-cover generation.
Existing rMAF partition and cut enumerators are appropriate development
instruments; fresh implementations are justified only for required lineage
separation or genuinely missing cover-generation functionality. Preserve
upstream validation scripts, integrity pins, original frozen sources/results
and alpha registrations. Use ./bootloops-python and ./bootloops-lean.

No mathematical import is unconditional because its progress card says
compiled or tested. Its exact hypotheses and final review provenance must be
traced at the consuming algorithm call site. In particular, triple-terminal
DP correspondence, pure-bridge graph realization and pending degree-two
interfaces remain open until their own gates pass.

The dashboard checkpoint is ../progress/algorithm.json. It distinguishes
Lean-checked statements, reviewed prose, finite executed tests and open
coverage/complexity obligations. Hosting, artifacts, compute and previews
remain local.
