The declared **source-level** bound O(N^4(b+1)), hence O(M^5), is supported under the explicit contract below. This is a manual mathematical audit of the actual released executable plus kernel-checked structural envelopes. It is not a Lean refinement theorem for compiled instructions, allocation, garbage collection or wall-clock time. The full algorithm remains OPEN.

Let N=treeSize T+treeSize S, b bound original-label bit length, and M be total explicit input bit length. Charge O(1) for Booleans, list constructors, and cost0/1 comparisons; O(b+1) for original Nat-label equality; linear address length for equality of original Boolean-address lists; and ordinary traversal/copying cost for lists. Count all potentially executed Boolean branches, so sharing and short-circuiting can only reduce this envelope.

The actual function is `Pretest.pretest01`, whose only candidate list is none followed by some p for every p in original source edges. The kernel proves the list length is treeSize S=2n−1, each edge is exactly a valid nonroot original address, and address length is O(N). Source construction maps prefixes/appends over at most N subtree vertices at each of at most N nodes, costing O(N^2). It never enumerates assignments, partitions, colors or source-core outputs at runtime.

The guard's two Nodup tests use the pinned Std pairwise decider: one recursive tail and one scan of that tail per head. Perm uses `List.isPerm`, one contains and erase per element. Both are O(N^2(b+1)), with O(N^2) conservative original leaves construction. The replay's two exact toolchain source pins were rechecked and the implementations read. This rules out assuming a factorial permutation decider behind the guard.

For a candidate, subtree lookup follows at most N original edges, and original leaves construction costs O(N^2) including append copies. Complement's literal filter predicate may recompute subtreeLabels for every source leaf; the audit deliberately charges all those recomputations, giving O(N^3+N^2(b+1)). Under the proper guard its two blocks have at most N labels altogether. Partition checks cost O(N^2(b+1)), even charging flatten rebuilding per count. Restriction visits each original node with a selection scan at each leaf, costing O(N^2(b+1)). Restricted trees have no more nodes than their originals.

`sameShapeCalls` charges all four possible recursive child comparisons. Its kernel theorem bounds this counter by the product of tree sizes. The manual connection to the literal original sameShape clauses gives O(N^2(b+1)) per restricted topology test. `minimalVisits` charges rootedSpan traversals and both possible recursive minimal children; its kernel theorem bounds that counter by N^2. Each charged node has at most O(N(b+1)) local selection/list work, so a minimal embedding costs O(N^3(b+1)). Each result contains at most N original addresses. The one bilateral pair per two-block source/target embedding check makes at most N^2 comparisons of addresses of length at most N, costing O(N^3). All suppressed original path vertices are counted.

The original `best` evaluates validity once per candidate and recurses on its tail once. Comparing its costs only compares0 and1. It does not call the proof-only source generator or reconstruct an unbounded forest family. Thus O(N) candidates times O(N^3(b+1)) work per candidate gives O(N^4(b+1)), in addition to smaller guard/construction work. N<=M and b<=M give the conservative O(M^5) bound for M>=1. The same allocation/traversal envelope yields polynomial space under the stated ordinary execution model.

The complete scientific definition-body closure from pretest01 has117 visited names. It contains the original forest constructor, original subtree traversal, checker, best, restriction, rooted/minimal embedding and sameShape functions. It excludes RMAF.assignments, RMAF.blocks, RMAF.solve, CoreProgram.generate, source_core and source_two_split. The standard callbacks are separately covered by the pinned Std-source audit. No implemented_by/extern execution override appears in the released scientific sources. Imported proof/control modules and their exhaustive four-label fixture checks do not lie in the executable dependency closure.

The kernel certifies candidate count/depth, the sameShape counter bound, and the minimalVisits counter bound. It does not certify all primitive-operation contracts or their coupling to extracted machine instructions. Source-level polynomial time is accepted only with this stated boundary. Kernel compilation time and the small measured control timings are not evidence for asymptotic runtime. No positive optimum/query-time discount, whole-state scheduler bound, recursive termination or faster global MAF base follows from this audit.
