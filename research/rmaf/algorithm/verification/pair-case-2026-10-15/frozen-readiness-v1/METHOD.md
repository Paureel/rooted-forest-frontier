# Fresh instrument scope and provenance

The pre-candidate mathematical contacts are the target pin and original
`RMAF.lean`, recorded with hashes in `source-contacts.jsonl`. No problem-specific
code was imported. The toolkit capability index contains no rooted phylogenetic
graph checker; numerical and algebraic tools there do not supply this task.
This is a one-shot verifier retained locally for the pair-case protocol.

The graph route explicitly builds every original address and edge, finds paths
by breadth-first search, and unions paths between selected leaf occurrences.
The topology route compares all nonempty selected descendant clusters, so the
root orientation survives while unary vertices are suppressed. These are
compared with separately written direct translations of the permitted original
definitions, not with any author implementation. They share the pinned tree
model and original semantics; independent agent identity is not replication
evidence. The evidence concerns their executed agreement at all tested finite
inputs. Candidate proof derivations and code have not been read.

Selections with absent or repeated labels have literal raw behavior but fail
the distinct-label theorem guards. A repeated leaf-labelled input may pass the
original executable agreement Boolean: `partitionCheck` does not itself enforce
distinct input leaves. The universal targets require distinct labels separately.

Controls run through the same entry point, serialization, graph, topology, and
partition functions as fixtures. Every literal comparator is paired with an
intentionally mismatched expected object. Fixture minima are over refinements
of the printed P; all Bell partitions form the stated outer denominator.

For each eligible edge and every refining agreement F, direct Fprime replaces
all A blocks by A and splits each final B block into its two descendant colors.
Every conclusion is checked separately, including full original agreement and
outside-block equality. Source one-cut witnesses concern only a final agreed
B block; no source cost or whole-parent reconstruction claim follows.

Unknown runtime is measured first on `binary_tiny`, with a 25-second cap per
fixture and at most seven labels/877 outer partitions. Outputs are completed
exact checks, literal denominator counts, and retained witnesses. A timeout,
unsupported interface, or failed assertion leaves the relevant scope OPEN.

The three-region fixture is a scoped probe, not a declared false plant. Its
unqualified existential exchange is evaluated from all refining agreements;
when true, it is reported true on that finite fixture. It supplies no negative
detector credit and no extension of E without its actual guard.

No universal G/E, complete algorithm, source generation/coverage, reconstruction
interface, or smaller-base claim is accepted by these finite calculations.
