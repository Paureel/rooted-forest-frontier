# Remaining obligations

The generic forest exact-minimum assembly has no additional graph or paid-disjoint premise. Its only mathematical callback assumption is the verbatim `OrdinaryIH` in `ForestProgram.lean`: every proper ordinary pair returns an affordable global minimum, and every affordable ordinary agreement guarantees a return. Constructing the full ordinary-tree dispatcher remains external work.

The qualified source bound consumes `LocalBillBound ordinary ordinaryWork T Q c K` in `ForestWork.lean`. Verbatim, this requires, for every literal source list ss and every `q ≤ Q` with `ForestState.proper T ss = true`,

    (nonrecursiveBill ordinary ordinaryWork T ss q : Rat) ≤ K*c^q.

`nonrecursiveBill` contains no recursive generic-forest work. It contains the printed inspection charge, actual ordered-pair target-span bill, completed source-core meter, concrete original-target color callback bills, every raw occurrence's replacement/materialization bill, final check/minimum bill, and exact metered paid ordinary calls. `source_work_bound` then proves the total recursive bound. A future source-cost review must justify the declared inspection charge against every primitive check, include scalar bit costs, and derive a polynomial K from a stated ordinary-tree work IH. Neither that coefficient derivation nor compiled-machine semantics is supplied by this package.
