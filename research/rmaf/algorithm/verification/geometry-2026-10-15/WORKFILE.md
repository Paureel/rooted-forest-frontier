unsuppressed graph vertices identified by binary leaf addresses and prefixes.

is empty; singleton hull is its leaf vertex. An original-label agreement
partition requires equal rooted restrictions and pairwise vertex-disjoint
minimal hulls in both original trees. k counts components and q=k−1 initially.
