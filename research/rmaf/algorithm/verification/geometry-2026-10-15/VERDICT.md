## Cost and cut semantics

The concrete selector's Lean cost fact counts candidate predicate evaluations,
not constant-time membership queries. `List.contains` scans span lists and
compares addresses; span/color construction and `eraseDups` also consume work.
The displayed definitions support a polynomial overhead argument on explicit
finite input trees. A complete formal operation bound and the entire solver's
charged recurrence are outside this release. No exponential source generator
is made polynomial by the selector theorem.

The wording in frozen `RESULT.md:59` and `Composition.lean:61–62` around
`distinct_parent_cut_cost` needs one precise qualification
when the coordinator updates the release status: **Nodup means distinct tagged
names, not nonredundant graph cuts**. `InSourceParent` checks only the parent
tag. Even distinct actual edges can isolate a leafless root component, as the
independent frozen payment control shows. A source certificate must separately
establish actual edge membership, nonempty regions, correct component increase,
and injective original-edge lifts. Same-parent unions still have no additive
cut-cost theorem here. This qualification leaves the proved list theorem intact.
