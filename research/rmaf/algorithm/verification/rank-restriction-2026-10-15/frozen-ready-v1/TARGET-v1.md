the original leaves. Rank feasibility means existence of an r-block original
partition passing partitionCheck and pairwiseEmbeddings, with monochromatic
blocks. It must contain no output/coverage/uniqueness/mass assumption.

The claimed source-program interface is: feasible inputs return the literal
empty output list below budget r-1, and exactly one literal output at every
budget q>=r-1. That output has r-1 actual original source cuts and every block
equals one entire original color class by membership. With infeasibility,

output costs at least L>=r-1, its raw LIST mass is at most

weight. All uses of proper original labels and actual generator membership,

An equivalent coverage-plus-dyadic-saturation proof is allowed; this target

## Restriction statements

Forward: original agreement F, proper original Q, and F refining Q give actual
local agreement on the literal restrictions to each U in Q. The local forest
is F filtered by containment in U. Prove actual local labels and the exact
sum_U(|F_U|-1)=|F|-|Q|; no term may hide an empty local family by Nat subtraction.
Q need not itself be an agreement forest or have target-disjoint embeddings
for this forward statement.

Reverse: from literal restriction equations, actual local agreement families,
proper Q in both original trees AND disjoint original Q embeddings in both,
construct the original agreement forest by flatMap and prove its summed cost
and label accounting. Original root/LCA and suppressed unary paths remain in
the embedding transport. Do not replace this by equality of restricted shapes
alone or assume the conclusion in an indexed-family record.

Requested clearing at one or two target vertices does not establish all Q
target embeddings disjoint. These theorems alone therefore do not license
reverse reconstruction on every actual source-core output or establish a
total recursive state, remaining-budget schedule, termination or runtime.
