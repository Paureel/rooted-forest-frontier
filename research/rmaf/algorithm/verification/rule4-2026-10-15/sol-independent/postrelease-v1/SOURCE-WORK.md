# Rule4 source work

The local source operations fit the qualified O(N^6 (L+B+1)^2) bound for proper admitted trees and bounded ExactChild returns. This conclusion concerns the selection and label-partition runner. The formal final recovery has a separate `finalPolynomial(S,G) * c^q` charge. The source counts below do not equate either program with a compiled instruction counter or measured wall time.

Here n is the common original leaf count, N=2n−1, L=1+the largest original Nat-label bit length, and B=1+bitlength(q). All Lean locators refer to the exact independently compiled files in `cold-source/`, whose original paths and hashes are in `source-dag.json`. Python locators refer to the released, pinned `research/rmaf/algorithm/universal233/rule4/rule4.py`. The proofs and numerical meters were read separately from the following loop counts.

## Finite sizes

A proper tree has N original vertices and n labels. Every address has at most N Boolean cells. Every accepted labelled partition has at most n blocks and exactly n label entries. A restriction to a nonempty block has at most twice its label count minus one constructors; therefore the sum of constructor counts in a valid materialized source forest is at most N. Each selected cut list has at most N heads, with two, one, and m≥2 heads in the three branches. Every original cherry yields two endpoint orders, and a distinct endpoint has only one actual sibling in each proper tree. Thus at most O(N) admissible Rule4 choices exist. The raw enumeration still scans every ordered target/source cherry pair and all rejected head pairs.

The largest counter for this local computation is polynomial in N, apart from the input/residual budget. Its bit length is O(L+B+1): n distinct Nat labels force the largest label to be at least n−1, so log N=O(L+1). Original address-cell scans are counted explicitly below, rather than priced as constant-length keys.

## Python helpers

The Python implementation performs explicit returned-data checks. `Graph` initialization (lines57–69) walks every original vertex and constructs original address strings. `embedding` and `pendant` (71–82) scan original paths and prefixes. `cut_regions` (88–110) builds original adjacency, traverses actual residual components, scans labels for each component and sorts their original vertices. `partition_after` (112–115) rejects leafless components. Every one of these operations was included; returned pointers are not counted as fresh certificates.

A deterministic implementation may use linear lists for set/dictionary operations. A lookup then scans at most N entries and compares full address strings of length at most N, or full label strings of encoded length at most L. Counting those comparisons directly gives O(N³(L+1)) for a two-endpoint original embedding/pendant scan and O(N^4(L+1)) for a complete original cut partition or original agreement check, conservatively. Sorting and all string/tuple copies are included. The n-label total across partition blocks prevents multiplying the full n-block and n-label bounds again for each block.

`candidates` (186–214) examines all target/source cherries and both endpoint orders. It materializes and checks all three branches of every admitted candidate before sorting/selecting, including the candidates later discarded. With O(N) admitted choices, O(N^5(L+B+1)^2) suffices for these Python operations under the conservative linear representation; the stated N^6 bound has room for the repeated scalar/copy accounting. `covering_exchange` (222–241) is proof/control-side code and is absent from the call chain of `select` and `solve_selected`.

`solve_selected` (276–306) checks every actual branch, affordability and residual budget, then each returned forest's labels, original topologies, both original embeddings, source refinement and paid identity. It also checks the original role reconstruction after a swapped callback. Each return has n total labels under ExactChild. There are at most three returns, with two original O(N^4(L+1)) agreement checks per return, and their copies remain within the bound. Python chooses a lexical forest on equal component count; Lean chooses the first equal minimum. Both satisfy their minimum theorem/contract, but no universal equivalence between the two programs is asserted.

Arbitrary enormous malformed callback data lies outside ExactChild. The source bound does not authorize reading unbounded foreign data for free. False refusals and nonminimum child answers also violate the supplied exact IH; neither implementation constructs that IH by mutual recursion here.

## Paid child and recovery work

For a selected branch with source partition P, `selected_source_state` proves |P|=paid+1, materialized source count=|P|, and each feasible child's |F|≥|P|. Integer arithmetic therefore gives paid+(|F|−|P|)=|F|−1 before Nat subtraction is used. For an original component limit k≥1, use ordinary cut budget q=k−1; the parent calls only affordable children at q−paid<q. `callbackMass` filters the actual LIST by affordability; duplicates contribute repeatedly. The uniform rational bound is c^−1+2c^−2 for c≥2. The independent exact examples and all actually affordable entries at c=2,233/100,3 are retained in the prerelease and actual Lean readouts.

`solveCuts` (`Rule4Solve.lean:29–30`) calls `FinalCutRecovery.recover S` once after minimum label selection. RecoveryProgram16–18 performs the original-source checks, runs the completed original generator at |G|−1 and takes its first output. Recovery27–49 proves the returned original certificate and exact cut count; the independent readouts checked complete original residual components. Local cut lists are never united.

`FinalWork.meterRecover_value` (78–81) binds the measured receipt to that literal recover call. The work bound (116–140) includes refused and pruned prefixes, actual receipt append cells, preparation and the block-color scan. `finalPolynomial` (109–111) includes the separate eighth-degree guard coefficient and the complete imported core cap. `solveCuts_recovery_work` (`Rule4Solve.lean:181–189`) charges this final call by finalPolynomial(S,G)c^q. The imported CoreWork quantities count source/list/address operations with explicit helper envelopes; encoded scalar costs retain their separate qualification. They do not prove a compiled-instruction or wall-time bound. This recovery term is separate from the local N^6 toll and from the callback mass. No complete global work induction is supplied.
