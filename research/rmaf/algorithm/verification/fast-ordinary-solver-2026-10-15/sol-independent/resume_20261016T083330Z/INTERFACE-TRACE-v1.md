`QueryExact` (`CommonDispatch.lean:42`) promises an agreement forest within q and no more components than any agreement forest, or excludes every forest of cut cost at most q. It promises minimum-answer semantics. It does not promise enumeration of every valid witness. `ExternalExact` (`ExternalRecovery.lean:11`) includes original source-cut realization, agreement, cuts+1=components, component budget, and global minimum. `RealizedOutput` (`Realization.lean:29`) quantifies over every original vertex, supplies exact component leaf membership and distinct roots, and excludes leafless components.

The source `Tree` (`RMAF.lean:8`) has binary nodes and Nat-labelled leaves. Empty/unary trees cannot be typed values; the JSON adapter refuses them. The new API uses arbitrary unique Nat labels (`Pretest.lean:34`), not the older `Instance` range-n convention. Rooted restrictions suppress unary vertices (`RMAF.lean:84`); isomorphism permits sibling swaps (`RMAF.lean:28`). The executable minimal embeddings retain original path vertices below the selected LCA (`RMAF.lean:126`), with the formal original-hull correspondence at `Bridge.lean:270` and `Bridge.lean:450`.
