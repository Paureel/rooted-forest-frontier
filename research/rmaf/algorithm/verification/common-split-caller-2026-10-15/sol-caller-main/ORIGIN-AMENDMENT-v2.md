# Prerelease driver extension

After the first clean pilot, while the candidate remains unopened, v2 checks that fresh Caller namespaces resolve to one of their actual stored producer owners. An incorrect but loaded Std origin must reject. General candidate resolved-origin fields still describe Lean's name-index origin, separately from actual producer/body identity; they are not silently promoted to proof-body ownership. The new detector already records both resolved and producer type/body/rules. The v2 driver also records its own/detector hashes and removes an unused false conditional.

The successful v1 pilot and driver remain. The small v2 pilot is rerun before full controls.
