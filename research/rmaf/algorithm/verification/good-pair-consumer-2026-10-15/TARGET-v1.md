existence of a NO-LARGER actual agreement Fprime and an actual emitted source
forest Q with Fprime refining Q and Q refining P. Q has source partition and
embedding validity, realized original cuts and cost d<=q. In the edge case A
is kept whole and rooted-good, and every B block is clear of A. In the vertex
case the output satisfies the specified vertex clearing alternative. The
literal same-list mass is <=c^-3+c^-4 for rational c>=2; the edge list has
the stronger c^-3 bound. Initial hard2 separation cuts are already paid.

Fprime is used in the correctness proof. The runtime generator must not ask
for F, Fprime, an optimum or an exchange oracle. Original F need not refine Q
after exchange; retaining that stronger assertion would be wrong. Neither Q
being an agreement forest nor a runtime bound for the new wrapper follows.
The additional hard-parent interface derives the source embedding premise
from literal original residual components, rather than assuming it.
