## Claims to test

1. On ordinary rooted binary original trees, actual source-generator outputs
   give proper original partitions with vertex-disjoint source embeddings,
   derived from the already reviewed original residual components.
2. Given an actual original agreement forest F refining a proper three-parent
   source partition P=[A,B,C], the literal whole-source clique, four-chain and
   two-joint-region diagonal generator call lists contain a Q with F refining Q
   and Q refining P, within the original cut budget and clearing the requested
   target vertices. This does not say Q is already a two-tree agreement forest.
3. The literal original-input coloring tags the parents by residues modulo
   three. Its distinct input-color count is the sum of their actual local
   counts. Actual original target-region and joint-region counts imply raw
   output-LIST mass bounds 3/c^4, 4/c^4, and c^-3+c^-4 respectively, for rational
   c>=2 and the printed geometry hypotheses. Multiplicity is retained. The
   parent tags already charge the two hard cuts; no extra c^-2 is allowed.
4. For an actual target cherry a,b and exactly two actual original source
   pendant edges on their path, the two fixed named cuts produce a proper
   three-component original partition P. Every original agreement F either
   contains singleton a, contains singleton b, or refines P. The named cuts
   must be chosen without knowing F, with sibling order and original vertices
   handled explicitly. No leafless residual component is allowed.

literal candidate list and exact failing inequality or predicate. A theorem
that is correct only under extra assumptions is OPEN at the exact consumer
unless that consumer derives them. Finite no-fire is not a universal proof.
Fresh kernel replay and an independently reconstructed semantic/interface
trace are both required for a scoped pass. Report missing bridges explicitly.
