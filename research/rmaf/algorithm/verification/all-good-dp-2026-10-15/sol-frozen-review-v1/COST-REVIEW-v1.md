# Source-operation review and correction finding

The final degree-six source-operation envelope can absorb repeated leaf-list construction, but the frozen cost document's displayed owner helper bound is false under its list/copy model. This is a cost-documentation finding, not a counterexample to agreement minimum, budget correctness or termination. The frozen author document is unchanged.

Read source: `all_good_dp/COST-MODEL.md:43–44` says, verbatim, “Across all target leaves this uses at most 4n(m+r+1) list/label operations.” The literal implementation at `ActualProgram.lean:6–7` is `ss.findIdx (fun s => (leaves s).contains x)`. `RMAF.lean:14–16` defines a node's leaves as `leaves l ++ leaves r`. Thus each owner query reconstructs source leaf lists. The model charges list constructors/copies; it does not posit a cached leaf list or compiler hoisting.

Take target and sole source to be the same left caterpillar with L distinct leaves. This is an accepted all-good state. One leaf-list construction copies 1+2+…+(L−1)=L(L−1)/2 left-list cons cells. There are L owner queries in the DP leaves. At L=64, target and source vertex counts are n=m=127 and r=1. Leaf construction alone copies129024 cons cells, exceeding4·127·(127+1+1)=65532. The independently frozen counter accepted L=1/3 known answers, observed these exact values, and rejected its declared wrong-count twin. This counter is a source append-copy accounting adapter, not a machine interpreter. Evidence: COST-CONTROL-EXPECTATIONS-v1, COST-CONTROL-MANIFEST-v1, cost-copy-control-v1.json. Inputs were frozen after candidate contact and are not represented as original blind READY controls.

A conservative replacement is4n(m²+m+r+1) unweighted owner operations. Each source leaves construction costs at most a constant times its squared vertex count; summing source squares is at most m². Membership/findIdx traversal adds at most a constant times m+r. This restores the source accounting without requiring a program change. Also, `ColorRegions.lean:20` spells `rawBlocks` with two separate `split p` calls; a source envelope should allow two reconstructions unless it explicitly assumes their sharing. Charging16(n+1)² instead of the document's8(n+1)² safely covers both calls.

The remaining source inspection supports the following coarse envelope under the document's explicitly stated primitive model. Set X=S+1, with S=n+m+r+b+1. Tree/list constructors, branches and pointer retention cost one; each Nat comparison/arithmetic/label-copy operation is conservatively charged S. No compiled-machine semantics is assumed.

* The guard constructs target/source leaf lists, checks Nodup and literal counts/memberships, performs restrictions and compares rooted shapes. Repeated flatten/list construction is charged.50X⁴ unweighted operations is conservative: leaf-list copying is at most quadratic in input vertices; count/membership scans are at most cubic; restriction visits and original-root-pair shape visits are at most quadratic before this coarse allowance. In `sameShape`, each call pair has a unique pair of original root addresses at equal depth; four pairings do not revisit the same original pair.
* The table's let-bound left/right results are evaluated once in this source model. Each internal row retains pointers to two chosen child paints. Each scan makes at most two score evaluations per entry. A score traverses at most n painted vertices and uses at most20n unweighted primitives. This gives80n²(r+1)², plus20n(r+1)² table/list and40n(r+1) root-selection primitives, as inspected at ColorDP.table/choose/edgeCost/changes and RMAF.best. The compiled table_length and visits_bound prove distinct row/count statements; neither includes score traversal or machine allocation.
* Corrected owner work is at most4n(m²+m+r+1)≤4X³. Two split reconstructions cost at most16X². Filter/output/list-length/budget work is covered by a further12X². Reconstruction retains empty raw regions until filtering; no optimization is rerun.

These contributions total at most130X⁴+24X³+68X² unweighted operations. Multiplying the whole expression by S also overcharges non-Nat primitives. Since S≤X and X≥1, it is at most222X⁵≤1000X⁶. This is an independent coarse mathematical source envelope with the two explicit corrections. It does not validate the frozen linear owner sub-bound. The counterexample and corrections must remain visible when the cost document is amended in a new version.
