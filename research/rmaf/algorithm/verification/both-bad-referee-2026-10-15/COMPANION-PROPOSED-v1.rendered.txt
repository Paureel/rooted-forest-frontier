The coverage result concerns every original agreement forest F refining P within the global cut budget. The retained extra unit is proved against that same original F using the actual local certificate (coverage and callback premises).


The global budget q counts final blocks minus one. A generated source partition Q uses d source cuts; its full entry has budget q-d and its retained entry has budget q-d-1, after the relevant guard. Every actual callback reduces q by at least 3. For each rational 2 ≤ c ≤ 3, assign a callback with child budget b the weight c^(-(q-b)). The sum of these weights over the actual callback trace is at most 4 c^(-4). Failed calls, duplicate occurrences, and calls before an early stop are counted. This weighted sum is a local recurrence contribution; it does not state that there are at most four calls or establish mutual termination (source cost model).


Full callbacks receive the whole current source forest. Retained callbacks receive the whole current B source forest. Source pieces within those forests may overlap on the target. The ordinary callback uses an explicitly target-disjoint restricted-pair interface. Source-only Q materialization must not be treated as target-Q agreement. Reconstruction uses the grouped forest contract; it does not union unrelated local cuts or replace a current B forest with the original whole B tree (forest interfaces).
