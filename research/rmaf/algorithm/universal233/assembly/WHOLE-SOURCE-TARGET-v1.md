# Whole-source ownership assembly

This is an OPEN assembly target. It changes how the new ownership rule calls
the actual generator; it does not repair the refuted pair certificate.

At an ordinary tree-tree call take the current input trees T,S themselves as
the original address graphs for that call. Let P=[A,B,C] be the literal source
parent partition induced by an admitted hard2 pendant branch before suppressing
addresses. Require source partition validity, the exact source branch safety
statement that some optimal final F refines P, and the original-label target
geometry. Those are named call hypotheses to derive; they are not inferred
from a sketch or included as a theorem's assumed conclusion.

For each ownership choice, color a label x by the pair consisting of its
source parent index and its required target deletion region. An untouched
parent has one local color. A once-cleared parent uses colorLabel T v. A
jointly cleared middle parent uses the pair of original target region colors
at v,w. Encode the finite pairs injectively as natural numbers. Every original
F-block belongs to one parent; the ownership cover and original-label
avoidance theorem imply that F is monochromatic for one such whole-source
coloring.

Call CoreProgram.generate on WHOLE S, at the remaining budget BEFORE paying
hard2 separately. Its actual output Q refines P by the distinct parent tags,
while F refines Q by the proved source coverage. Its recorded original cut
addresses are all in S and its actual original components are Q. This avoids
composing independently suppressed parent cut addresses back into S. No
same-parent cut union or assumed cut-subset inclusion is used.

If the local color counts are r_A,r_B,r_C, the whole count is their sum.
The raw mass bound is c^(1-r_A-r_B-r_C), exactly the initial hard2 factor
c^-2 times the product of the three per-parent factors c^(1-r_i).
Three clique ownership branches with two cleared binary parents each have
at least5 colors and total mass at most3/c^4. The four chain alternatives
with at least3 joint middle regions each have at least5 colors; count-two
coincidence uses one middle clearing versus two outer-parent clearings.
Exact empty/nonempty color counts and paid diagonal cases must match the
geometry theorems; an empty parent is not a nonempty color.

For a stronger minimum emitted cost L and r total colors, the proved dyadic
mass gives, at c>=2,

    sum c^-cost <= 2^(L-(r-1)) * c^-L.

This is useful when a concrete source-color lower bound is stronger than r-1.
A polynomial Fitch/source-color query could establish L, but its graph and
implementation interface must be proved. No lower bound is assumed because
a local query itself remains unresolved.

The exact14 pilot WHOLE-SOURCE-PIN-v1.json executes this construction at all3
shared vertices: whole-source A costs4,4; whole-source B cost3. Their full
mass remains2962867400/2947295521>1. Thus the construction is an integration
route only. Root's separate pair-case selector/exchange route must repair
that case; this document is not a competing proof task for those authors.

The first assembly proof is: original agreement F refining the actual P,
plus the proved original vertex ownership/region predicates, yields a member
of the literal whole-source generated output list preserving original F and
clearing the required target vertices, with realized original cut cost and
raw mass. Afterward, derive P/refinement from the actual source priority
history and compose the unchanged source rules, complete termination and
reconstruction. Per-prefix polynomial implementation work is under a separate
proof. All3 main milestones stay OPEN.
