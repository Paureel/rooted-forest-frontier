# First consuming call not yet assembled

The canonical `clique_forest_cover`, `chain_forest_four_cover`, and `chain_forest_diagonal_cover` calls require `partitionCheck (leaves source) [A,B,C] = true` and `RefinesBlocks F [A,B,C]`. These properties are proved by the separately frozen hard-parent safety theorem on the no-endpoint-singleton branch of an actual target cherry with exactly two original source pendants. Version 1 does not yet import that theorem or construct the outer singleton-versus-hard-child disjunction. The next explicit consuming call is therefore an original `hard2_forest_cover` that obtains the named cuts, handles `[a]` or `[b]` membership, and feeds only its refinement alternative into these forest constructors.

After that local composition, the complete algorithm still needs actual prior-rule/state selection and reconstruction. In particular, a returned source forest may contain further target overlaps; clearing the requested vertices does not make every output an agreement forest. At the recursive forest-processing call, one must construct exact restricted subproblems, prove the remaining-budget identities and agreement reconstruction, pay all capped local-query work (including p=1), and prove termination and the full recurrence. None of these properties is an assumption licensed by `ForestBranchSolution`.

The literal mass of the generated call lists is proved in a separate frozen author package, and actual color construction/whole-source work is being proved separately. Their exact definition match to this assembly and their consuming runtime inequality have not yet been composed here. All downstream complete-algorithm and smaller-base conclusions remain conditional. All three main milestones remain OPEN.
