leaf labels. Let a≠b form an ACTUAL unsuppressed cherry in T: some original

the original directed child edges(p,c) where p lies on that path and c does not.

be the literal three residual original leaf sets, with C containing a,b.
Prove actual nonempty components/no leafless regions; their ordered names do
not matter to refinement.

    [a] is a block of F;

    F refines P (each literal original F block lies in one P block).
