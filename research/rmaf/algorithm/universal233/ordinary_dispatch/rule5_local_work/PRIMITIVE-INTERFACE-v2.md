actual_common_bounded_polynomial consumes the checker bill only on G with
  cells G <=12*leaves(d.T).length+1
  every label z in G.flatten satisfies z<2^(L+2).
Both premises are derived from the actual successful buildSetup, closed FX/FM
callbacks, actual found-one FY and actual outsideReply FN. The original input
premise is all labels of T and S <2^L. No uniform checker bill over unbounded
numeric labels is required. A checker value binding to actual RMAF.agreement on
literal candidates still supplies reconstruct_value separately.

commonPolynomial(2n,K) is the explicit uniform reconstruction structural bill;
it removes |FM| and |FN| through their proved payload caps. The theorem includes
both stable and unstable reconstruction branches and all literal checker calls.

not desired whole-caller work fields. Machine/bit correspondence and the
selectLocal->admitted first gap remain OPEN.
