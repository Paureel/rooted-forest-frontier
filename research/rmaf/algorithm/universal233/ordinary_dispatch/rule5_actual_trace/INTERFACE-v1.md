# Actual Rule5 source trace interface v1

Import WorkBinding. Namespace: Universal233.Rule5ActualTrace.

`dispatchTrace actual T S a b q : Receipt` binds to the original actual program.
`dispatch_value` proves its value equals Rule5OrdinaryDispatch.dispatch;
`rule5_value` proves its flattened answer equals
OrdinaryDispatch.Hard2Dispatcher.rule5Answer. These value theorems need no
ordinary IH or geometry/minimum/cover premise.

The Calls alternative is literal. Prefix/first/third store their actual
affordable forestJobs, execute GenericForest.solve at each full state and
select the checked original best. Common.one stores DIRECT ordinary jobs;
commonTrace reproduces buildSetup, outside0/1 checks, outsideReply, paidJobs,
both callback evaluations, reconstruction and final budget guard. common_value
and common_calls bind those evaluations to the frozen common source primitive
and its commonJobs. Paid.second runs actual buildSelected, then every literal
affordable request/currentAnswer evaluation, including full source forests and
complete originalCuts. Selector/paid-builder refusal remains a tagged result.
The raw CommonReceipt also retains outer common refusal; common_raw_nonrefusal
proves it cannot occur on the actual selected common.one case.

`queries actual receipt.calls` is the actual source-level ordinary frontier.
Forest jobs use frozen GenericForest.FrontierWork.frontier. Direct jobs map
to their actual ordinary inputs/budgets. Paid queries use currentFrontier:
pretest-resolved/refused states and rejected residual<=1 states have no ordinary
callback; hard rejected states use the full unchanged forest frontier. Their
outer requests remain recorded even when no ordinary callback follows. This
uses the completed source-level frontier guard correspondence, not a compiled
machine-callgraph or trace-instrument execution identity.

Every original list occurrence is retained. Failed answers do not delete the
corresponding call record; the forest scheduler stops only where its actual
source program stops. Direct common jobs both execute even when one fails.
No duplicate elimination, cheap replacement of a whole overlapping forest,
free cut/refund or conversion of common direct jobs into forest jobs occurs.

`dispatch_mass` proves the ACTUAL ordinary frontier mass is at most gamma(base),
where base=233/100 and gamma(c)=2*c^-1+4*c^-4. `dispatch_mass_strict` proves it
is below1. No OrdinaryIH or desired mass field is a premise. The inner forest
frontier bound comes from the sealed source theorem, not an assumption.
Prefix [2]/[2,2,2,2,2] and first/third [1,2] use complete outer debits. Common
uses its two direct q-1 jobs. Paid costs are the ACTUAL [2,1,2,2] or [2,1,d]
with d>=2, conservatively bounded by c^-1+3*c^-2 before comparison to gamma.
Concrete three-cut and four-cut states remain fully charged; no relabeling of
their costs is used. All deeper forest debits are retained by the inner trace.

`dispatch_query_bounds` proves every actual ordinary call is proper, has r<q,
and BOTH queried trees have at most the old target label COUNT. The direct
marked common query restricts a real representative then renames it, preserving
that count. It may introduce a fresh numeric label. `dispatch_potential` gives
n'<=n+2 and n'+2r<=n+2q for both queried trees, with the stronger n'<=n result
available. Numeric label maxima/bit lengths are separate, unproved here.

`ordinaryWork` is the sum of supplied ordinary charges over the actual source
frontier. `dispatch_ordinary_work_bound` proves a D*base^q*gamma bound when
each ACTUAL emitted callback charge is at most D*base^r. This is the ordinary
recursive bill, not total caller work or a desired mass premise. Selector,
builder, pretest, failed/refused source work, local forest overhead, payload,
freshmax, address/bit arithmetic and compiled-machine accounting remain open.

No selected Rule5 value/trace/mass/strict-budget/label-count interface remains
unproved in this component. The next consuming source call is the total
ordinary recurrence's concrete nonrecursive Rule5 source bill and its numeric
label/bit envelope. All main/base/novelty/independent acceptance remains OPEN.
