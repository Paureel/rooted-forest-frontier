# Grouped full-state source work v1

Runtime definitions use original trees, Nat labels, label groups and literal
source lists. Each original group runs the frozen universal full-forest pretest
once. Resolved forests retain their exact full-state additional cost; hard
jobs retain the entire current source list and call the unchanged GenericForest
solver with the original actual ordinary callback. No runtime minimum function,
completion ghost, Classical.choose or optimum oracle was introduced.

The existing scheduler pays every other known cost and every other hard lower
bound before assigning its uniform residual. The actual trace and any early
failure are proved to satisfy the paid mass bound when at least two actual
groups have nonzero cost. This does not bound a sole hard group by c^-1.
The actual source partition's precuts are paid separately at P.length-1;
original-grouped correctness is conditional on the stated source geometry.

All144 immutable source/olean dependency pairs are hardlinks, separately
pinned and never rebuilt or modified. New source compiles only in owned
build-v3 or new result directories. Initial inventory/partial-link refusals
were retained; fresh build paths preserve earlier immutable links untouched.

The frozen pretest's original-edge candidate enumeration, original source
realization, forest checker and representation qualifications carry over.
Tree and forest payload copying, labels and vertex-address sizes retain their
actual costs. Arbitrary Nat arithmetic requires its true bit cost. No complete
primitive-instruction, compiled-machine or whole-algorithm recurrence bill is
proved here. The scheduler's inherited source-model bill is conditional;
preparation and payload work must be charged by the eventual total consumer.
