## Local graph statements

`CoreGraph.SourcePartition` (`CoreGraph.lean:189–195`) requires actual vertices, exact terminal ownership, connected walks and disjoint supports. It has no coverage, contraction, cut-realization or mass field. Supports may contain extra vertices or have no terminal, so checking only minimal leaf spans would have missed part of the delivered statement.

`mixed_cherry_singleton` (`CoreGraph:230–242`) proves that at least one differently colored cherry child is alone among current leaf occurrences in its block. If both children had another leaf in their blocks, connectedness would put their common parent in both supports. Disjointness would equate the block owners, contradicting monochromaticity and the unequal colors. `unique_color_singleton` (`:246–251`) proves its conclusion directly from monochromaticity and explicit uniqueness among current leaf occurrences. These propositions establish local singleton alternatives; applying them after a program deletion requires a graph transport theorem.

`homogeneous_cherry_coarsening` (`CherryCoarsening:196–204`) gives a partition on the same actual unsuppressed Tree. It merges the two cherry owners and preserves owner equality in the refinement direction defined at `:158–160`. The support construction (`:13–16`) unites both cherry supports with the parent, empties the second support and removes the parent from all other supports. A third block may occupy that parent. Its remaining support stays connected because it can contain neither cherry leaf, leaving at most one neighbor of the parent in that support (`:56–71`, `:110–114`, `CoreGraph.Walk.avoid_one_neighbor:78–101`). The proof permits a root cherry and retained degree-two vertices. It does not construct the grouped normalized graph.
