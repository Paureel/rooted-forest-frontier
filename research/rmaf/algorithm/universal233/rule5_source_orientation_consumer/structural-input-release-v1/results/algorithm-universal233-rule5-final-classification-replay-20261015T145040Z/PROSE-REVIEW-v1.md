test, then made a separate second pass for scope and provenance. The text names
the actual source/target hypotheses, the uniform graph conclusions and the
unconsumed recursive implication. Finite controls are labelled development;
declaration counts are inventory. No independent, complete-algorithm, smaller
base, novelty or paper-defect claim is made. Parent-coordinated fresh review is
still required; this author check supplies no independent credit.
