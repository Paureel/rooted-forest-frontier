## Structural controls and alternatives

A source profile is not a target agreement certificate. A contracted homogeneous group may contain an arbitrary rooted pair with positive further cut cost. Profile [1] may have arbitrarily many normalized groups: T_m is the rooted caterpillar with one A leaf at the deepest cherry and m B leaves on its spine. For every m>=1 it emits [1]. False twin: interpreting this profile as a bound of at most three normalized groups must fail at m=4. This is a concrete obstruction to finite raw-group census; any finite compression must quotient attachments or retain them as variables.

The structural objective is to classify original-label target spans and actual local-query lower bounds, keeping p=1 loss zero admissible. A profile-only prediction of extra loss is a deliberate false claim and must not pass by arithmetic alone.
