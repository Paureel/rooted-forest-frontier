## Exact local statements

All forests use the thesis's labelled unary rho convention. Work with finite reduced binary current forests F1,F2 on the same labels, each F2 component contained in an F1 component. A,C are complete proper common pendant subtrees rooted at a,c; a,c are siblings in F1 and connected in F2. E consists of actual current F2 edges and yields an AF. An explicit E2 subset E preserves A,C intact and makes a,c siblings after yielding. Let P be their path, u its LCA, and D=E intersect P.

**Restricted normalization N_anchor.** For |D|=1, if the endpoint fragment detached from u is nonempty and u's component contains an outside label, require an A/C label connected to u. No anchor is required when |D| differs from one. Remove every edge of D. If |D| is at least two, add the incoming edge of u only when that edge actually exists. The result is an AF and its cardinality is |E|-|D|+delta, where delta records a genuinely new incoming cut. Nonempty root fragments at both endpoints are a sufficient stronger premise. Compound endpoints and internally cut endpoint subtrees are allowed. This AF theorem is reviewed prose.

**Strict empty-root repair.** In a proper identical common subtree whose root has no connected descendant label after E, restore all internal cuts and cut its incoming edge. At least two internal cuts are removed and at most one edge added; the result is an AF of strictly lower cost. Therefore a global minimum cutset on the current F2 retains a labelled root fragment at every such common-subtree root. Minimum among forced extensions or inclusion-minimality does not supply this conclusion. This is also reviewed prose.

**Specific corridor transport.** Suppose x is the original sibling of u and B consists of exactly two pendant edges attached at internal vertices of P. Cutting B and yielding suppresses precisely two arm vertices; changed non-B edges lie on P. For every K containing B and avoiding P, C=K minus B is a set of unchanged actual edges of G=yield(F2 minus B), and yield(G minus C)=yield(F2 minus K), with identical surviving vertex IDs, rooted endpoints, labels, root fragments at u,x and whole-component labels there. The exact count is |K|=2+|C|. The second x-u path is literally the original ex,eu, and the optional incoming edge of their parent retains its endpoints or remains absent. Full second normalization therefore lifts by reattaching B with the same yield and additive count. This transport needs no AF or optimality premise. Its graph formulation is reviewed prose; its formal counterpart assumes explicitly witnessed splices.
