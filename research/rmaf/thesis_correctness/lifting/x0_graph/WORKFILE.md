The ten-graph pilot took 0.291605125 seconds. Early/late means differed because graph edge count increases through the enumeration, so the naive constant per-graph extrapolation was superseded before launch. `timing-n4-stratified.json` uses the actual n=4 edge-count distribution and sampled per-stratum costs; missing strata use a larger sampled edge-count cost. Conditional projection 4.989965090 seconds, checkpoint cap 45 seconds. Real progress = completed graph indices plus C/R/K/origin denominators in the atomic receipt.

The full n=4 run completed 141/141 graphs in 4.475399333 seconds.
