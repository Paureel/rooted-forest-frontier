Once e collapses, the proof must account for the final `(a,e)` call and its child before assigning empty analysis credits.

> Let J be a first three-child G4.1 A3BC call reached from an unprotected top-level instance by the source rules, with incoming P={a}, Q empty and exactly three pendants. If the active e fringe has three or four roots, let b0 be a's sibling subtree at the original G1 A2BC C cut. Then a is still the same active root, b0 has a nonempty surviving representative b that remains a's sibling in F2, both incoming attachments of a and b are protected, and each endpoint of J's selected deepest pair is outside b. Every reduction used to establish this statement must preserve source priority, ordered endpoints, simultaneous-cut legality and the active representative.

The present source argument and finite local checks support this lemma but do not formally encode or exhaust its histories.
