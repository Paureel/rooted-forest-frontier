Exact minimal embedding from the unchanged extractor¶ ↑

LeastEmbedding.embedding constructs BridgeReview.MinimalEmbedding from an actual tree t : RMAF.Tree, a selection S, and witnesses x ∈ leaves t and x ∈ S. Its support list is exactly RMAF.minimalVertices S t. Its ambient and terminal lists have proved interpretations as actual vertex addresses and selected leaf occurrences. Ambient inclusion, terminal preservation and leastness are proved; none is a constructor argument. The author audit kernel-checks this interface. The independent semantic verdict is recorded separately in acceptance.json and the fresh review receipts.

Mathematical statement and construction¶ ↑

An address is a finite Boolean list, with false for a left child and true for a right child. ValidVertex t v is the inductive root/child occurrence relation. LeafOccurrence t v x is the inductive leaf relation, retaining both address and label. TreeAddresses.lean proves

  • membership in vertices t is equivalent to ValidVertex t v;

  • membership in terminals S t is equivalent to an actual leaf occurrence at that address with a label in S;

  • a label belongs to leaves t exactly when it has a leaf occurrence;

  • two leaf occurrences at the same address have the same label; each label occurring in leaves t has exactly one address when leaves t has no repeated labels;

  • each permutation field of RMAF.Instance supplies this uniqueness for every label below Instance.n, and excludes other labels.

Arbitrary repeated-label trees are included in the constructor theorem. Selecting such a label selects every occurrence. Repeated entries in the selection do not repeat terminal addresses. vertices_nodup and terminals_nodup are proved for all trees and selections. Empty or entirely absent selections have empty output and do not satisfy the constructor's explicit occurring-selected-label premise. A selected singleton-root leaf is included.

ExtractorLeastness.lean proves by structural recursion that every rooted-span vertex has a selected terminal descendant, all selected terminals survive minimalVertices, and every emitted vertex is an actual vertex. It also proves that any common prefix of all selected terminals is a prefix of every emitted minimal vertex. In the two occupied child branches this common prefix is empty; in a single occupied branch the recursive argument retains that child's address prefix. No unproved equality with a separately defined hull is imported.

For a competing ConnectedSupport, its already proved graph-to-region map gives a root above all its vertices and convexity along ancestor paths. Its root is therefore a common ancestor of the selected terminals. Given an emitted vertex v, choose a selected terminal below v. The common-ancestor lemma places the competitor's root above v; its convexity places v in the competitor. This is minimal_least. It holds for every connected competitor containing the selected terminals, even without requiring that competitor to stay in the input ambient tree. It therefore supplies the narrower ambient-bounded minimal field.

The constructor uses the previously audited extractedSupport for connectivity and exact support-list preservation. embedding_region_exact identifies the existing MinimalEmbedding.region membership with the unchanged extractor. embedding_consumer calls the original MinimalEmbedding.deepest_root_in_neighbor, retaining its depth and overlap hypotheses, and returns exact extractor membership. Equality of coordinates from different input trees is only equality of address lists; this theorem does not establish a correspondence between those trees.
