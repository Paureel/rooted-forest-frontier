The local and global requirements differ. Neither the one-tree construction nor the intended two-tree transport needs identical full label sets outside B. The local target does require every selected B label to occur in both trees, unique labels in each tree, and agreement of the B restrictions. Whole-partition replacement starts with the original `agreement` predicate, including both full partition checks. An actual `RMAF.Instance` additionally supplies canonical matching full label lists and unique labels. The Boolean predicate alone does not imply uniqueness of all tree leaves.

The remaining obligations at `agreement_replace` and `agreement_replace_and_clear` are precise. For the actual split, prove `restrictionAgrees t u b0 = true`, `restrictionAgrees t u b1 = true`, and disjointness of the two actual u supports. The four support containments can already be derived from label inclusion using `actual_support_mono`, independently in each input tree. The one-tree theorem already gives nonemptiness, permutation, t disjointness and t avoidance. No cross-tree equality of addresses is needed or justified: the root and suppressed-path fixtures have different addresses for the same leaf labels in t and u.

For G3's component statement, `actualDegree_propagation` supplies exactly the component-to-block degree step when B is contained in S, v lies in B's support and v is not a selected B leaf occurrence. Applying a component theorem still requires proving that last premise in its intended forest setting. For G4, `pairwise_other_avoids` handles the actual before/after list occurrences, so it does not assume block-name uniqueness. The preservation theorem remains conditional on the local cross-tree facts above. A `clearRest` instance additionally needs an explicit remainder type, validity predicate carrying all old marks/interactions, avoidance predicate, cost definition, and the correspondence between that forest representation and executable partitions. The already-avoiding case keeps the original state; the occupied case must locate the unique affected piece and satisfy its degree premises. G2 and G5–G7 are outside this review.
