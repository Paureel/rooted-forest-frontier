# Erased proof support

`minimumCallback` and `ghost` are explicitly noncomputable Lean definitions.
They use classical choice from a minimum theorem proved by Nat strong
induction. Every use of ghost outside its own definition occurs in
theorem statements, proofs or propositional controls. No #eval, native_decide, extracted executable, callback
implementation or actual solve/disjoint/schedule program uses the ghost.

The actual callback's complete result is preserved on every pair at every
b≤K. No properness filter, output normalization or replacement changes those
values. Above K, the ghost may differ, as a retained control proves.

The isolated build verifies source/kernel declarations. It does not execute
the ghost, prove executable totality or establish a source/bit/payload cost
model. No source-work claim is made. The final receipt's byte and producer
correspondences are verification evidence only, subject to its recorded
Lean runtime and detector scope.

The completed forest v3 inventory predates the stored-producer amendment;
its old resolved-name qualification is retained. This release runs the
required stored-producer audit on its entire 79-module scientific dependency
closure. It makes no fresh all-module audit claim for excluded v3 modules.
