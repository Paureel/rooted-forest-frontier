The literal raw caps are established before acceptance. A pretest has exactly `treeNodes S` candidates; every forest has at most two blocks and at most `2*treeNodes S` label cells. Every candidate address is at most `treeNodes S` long. Cherry candidates number at most `treeNodes T`; each pendant list is at most `treeNodes S` long. Rule4 ordered cherries number at most twice the tree node count. The actual raw guard restricts each inner q/r output to four choices, so rawChoices has at most `16*(N+1)^2` entries. `rawChoice_head` derives every raw cHead bound from source enumeration membership, including rejected entries. Vertex equality traverses at most the bounded first address, so the second comparison address need not be bounded by an accepted-guard premise.

Each structural operation has an explicit fixed fee in its source companion. Variable-length traversals have recurrences, not unit oracle fees. Nat/Bool equality and order, constructor/branch slots, increments, counter arithmetic and label copies remain scalar unit slots. Pair equality expands into two Nat equality slots. The std Nat-list and vertex-list Perm procedures expand contains/erase recursion. Imported checker primitives retain the completed checker's scalar qualification. There is no binary/native execution claim for any of these slots.
