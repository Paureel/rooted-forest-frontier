# Contributor conduct

Treat contributors respectfully. Critique claims and evidence, provide precise
counterexamples, and preserve credit for predecessors, reviewers and useful
failed attempts. Make the scope of disagreements clear and avoid personal
attacks, harassment, or sharing another person's private information.

The maintainer, Aurel Prosz (@Paureel), moderates project collaboration. Raise
conduct concerns with the maintainer directly rather than copying private
details into a research issue.
