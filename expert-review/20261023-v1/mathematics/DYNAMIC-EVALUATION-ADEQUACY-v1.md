## Why the chronology contains every actual invocation

The construction proves exhaustiveness simultaneously with its return assertion. Closed cases contribute the empty word. A D0..D3 terminal contributes exactly its actual one attempted entry and its observed live/returned values. No child body exists on an actual cutoff refusal. Sequential actual subevaluations concatenate their entire words; a stopped actual branch contributes no unevaluated tail. Structural cons construction never merges entries when their data match. Consequently every actual immediate QUERY body is the live entry of one terminal site and is present once, and every live graph entry is one such actual body. This is a position correspondence over the entire event word, stronger than membership of each known query in a list.

For linear constructors the corresponding graph word is their literal slots list. For Rule4 and full outputs, tail-first evaluation derived the reverse-cell chronological word in the construction above. For PaidUnits it derived the paid-before-units word. Labels use the actual input ordinal and inner attempt ordinal, so their chronological positions identify every duplicate separately. For disjoint and all remaining composites the word is the explicitly derived head/block concatenation. These statements are conclusions of the actual-subvaluation induction, not assumptions that graph SlotRealizes already implies chronology. SlotRealizes by itself has no exhaustiveness conclusion.

Refused PAID attempts remain entries in the attempt word, with live=false, returned=None and their actual local comparison/return operations. Filtering only live entries therefore gives exactly the actual body word. A live failed child stays live with returned=None; it is not removed as a refusal. The actual terminal return used at every graph premise is the actual child return from the smaller execution. All retained scheduler pair costs are constructed by their displayed map/additionalCost after that return; no cost answer is supplied.
