Measure used in this correction.
Count unfolded finite tag/field/list occurrences, including constructor tags, scalar words, list head/tail slots and every terminating nil. Repeated immutable references in a collection are unfolded separately for this upper bound, even when physically shared. A Nat or Bool is one word occurrence here; operand bit widths and activation-frame realization remain outside this lemma. The implementation is not asked to evaluate this measure.

A path/list of l scalar entries has <=4*(l+1) occurrences:3 per cons and its finalnil. A Forest with B blocks and f flattened scalar entries has <=4*(B+f+1):3B+1 for the outer spine and3f+B for its inner spines. A typed tree/Paint node is bounded by32 local tag/field occurrences, including the finite constructor record layout. A fixed record with<=20 computational fields has<=128 local tag/field/terminal occurrences, excluding the contents of its referenced fields, which are counted below. These bounds conservatively dominate the frozen layouts.

Cut the generator-derived output/entry/query/calls/refusal/candidate spines at their explicit occurrence/copy boundary. Their individual elements have the finite bounds below, but the complete unbounded spines and the receipts pointing to them have no D' claim. Their traversals remain paid by the generator/forest/outer-list source channels. Thus this correction does not turn an exponential list into a cubic one. The bounded merge-candidate collection is a different, finite Cartesian product.
